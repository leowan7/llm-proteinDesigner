# Plan 10-01: Legal Content Drafting

## Objective

Draft v1 of the four legal documents as static React components under `frontend/src/pages/legal/`. Each page carries a `LEGAL_VERSION` constant that downstream plans (10-02 acceptance gate, 10-04 GDPR endpoints) reference to enforce re-acceptance on revisions.

## Scope (what this plan delivers)

1. **Terms of Service** (`frontend/src/pages/legal/Terms.tsx`)
   - Acceptance scope, service description, account responsibilities
   - IP ownership clause: user retains all rights to uploaded structures and generated designs
   - No-training / no-sharing clause: Kendrew does not use user-uploaded structures for model training or share with third parties
   - Acceptable use: no attempts to extract proprietary algorithms, no circumvention of rate limits, no sharing of account credentials
   - Service tier + payment terms (pay-per-GPU-second via Stripe)
   - Liability limitations, warranty disclaimer, indemnification
   - Termination, governing law (Ontario, Canada), arbitration clause for international users
   - Changes to terms — notification + re-acceptance flow
   - Contact for legal notices

2. **Privacy Policy** (`frontend/src/pages/legal/Privacy.tsx`)
   - Data we collect: account info (email, hashed password), content (PDB uploads, job specs, conversations), metadata (timestamps, IP for rate limiting only)
   - How we use it: provide the service, generate designs, send job notifications, prevent abuse
   - Subprocessors: link to /legal/subprocessors
   - Data retention: jobs + structures auto-expire after 90 days from creation (configurable 30-365)
   - GDPR rights (Articles 15-22): access, rectification, erasure, portability, objection, restriction — all exercisable from settings
   - CCPA rights: disclosure, deletion, opt-out of sale (we do not sell)
   - Security measures: TLS in transit, encryption at rest, HTTP-only cookies, RLS on user tables
   - Children: service not intended for users under 16
   - International transfers: data processed in US/EU via subprocessors; Standard Contractual Clauses where applicable
   - Contact: privacy@ranomics.com
   - Effective date + version

3. **Subprocessors** (`frontend/src/pages/legal/Subprocessors.tsx`)
   - Table of 8 subprocessors with: name, service, data handled, region, privacy link
   - Notification of changes: 30 days advance notice via email + posted here
   - "Last updated" date

4. **Cookie Policy** (`frontend/src/pages/legal/Cookies.tsx`)
   - Cookie inventory: access_token, refresh_token, csrftoken (all strictly-necessary)
   - No analytics, no advertising, no third-party tracking cookies
   - Consent banner scope (informational only since strictly-necessary cookies don't require opt-in under GDPR/CCPA)
   - Re-open consent via footer link

## Decisions embedded in this plan

- **Content format: .tsx components, not MDX.** Rationale: minimal dependencies (no @mdx-js/react), consistent styling with Tailwind, avoids build-config changes. Content is structured HTML inside the component — Claude-readable, reviewable, and diff-friendly.
- **Single `versions.ts` module** as the source of truth for version strings. Plan 10-02's signup gate and login re-acceptance check read from here.
- **"Draft — legal review pending" banner** is a React component at the top of each page. Removed when counsel signs off (tracked as a separate commit).
